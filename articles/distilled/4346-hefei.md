# Hefei

## Overview
Hefei is the capital and political, economic, and cultural center of Anhui province, China. Historically an administrative and market town, it transformed into a major industrial and high-tech hub in the late 20th and early 21st centuries, becoming a leading global city for scientific research and innovation.

## Body
### History and Development
Hefei's history spans from ancient states to its role as a contested border region between northern and southern dynasties. The present city was established during the Song dynasty. Before World War II, it was primarily an administrative center and regional market. Its modern growth began after it became the provincial capital in 1945, followed by industrial development in the 1950s. A major transformation was triggered in 2005 by a strategy prioritizing automobile, electric appliance, and equipment manufacturing, alongside aggressive investment recruitment and infrastructure construction. This led to its emergence as a high-tech and innovation-driven economy, particularly in semiconductors and alternative energy.

### Economy and Investment
Hefei's economy is characterized by active government investment and state-owned enterprises. A pivotal 2008 investment in BOE Technology helped establish Hefei as a major center for the optoelectronic industry. The government also invested in firms like iFlytek (voice recognition) and NIO (electric vehicles), the latter moving its headquarters to Hefei in 2020. This approach has driven growth in high-tech sectors, including memory chip manufacturing. The city's GDP was CN¥1,267.38 billion in 2023.

### Geography and Climate
Located in central Anhui, Hefei sits north of Chao Lake, one of China's largest freshwater lakes, and on a saddle crossing the Dabie Mountains. It has a humid subtropical climate with four distinct seasons, hot and humid summers, and damp, cold winters. Air quality can be affected by agricultural field burning in May and June.

### Transportation and Infrastructure
Hefei is a natural transportation hub. It has an extensive rail network, including high-speed lines, and the Hefei Xinqiao International Airport (opened 2013). A rapid transit metro system, the Hefei Metro, has been under construction since 2016, with multiple lines operational.

### Universities and Research
Hefei is a leading global city for scientific research, ranking 12th worldwide in the 2025 Nature Index. It hosts seven national laboratories and numerous research institutes, many under the Chinese Academy of Sciences. Major universities include the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), a top-100 global research university, and Hefei University of Technology. The city is home to the Experimental Advanced Superconducting Tokamak, a magnetic fusion energy reactor.

## Terms
- **Prefecture-level city:** An administrative division in China that governs surrounding counties and districts.
- **Third Front construction:** A Chinese government initiative (1960s-1980s) to develop industrial and military infrastructure in the country's interior.
- **High-Tech Industrial Zone:** A designated area focused on developing and attracting technology-based industries.
- **Nature Index:** A database that tracks research output and author affiliations, used to rank cities and institutions for scientific research.
- **DRAM:** Dynamic Random-Access Memory, a type of semiconductor memory used in computers.
- **Optoelectronic industry:** An industry combining optics and electronics, including products like displays and sensors.
- **Superconducting tokamak:** A device that uses powerful magnetic fields to confine plasma for nuclear fusion research.
