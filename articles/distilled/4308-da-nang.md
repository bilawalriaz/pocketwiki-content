# Da Nang

## Overview
Da Nang is a major port city and one of Vietnam's five direct-controlled municipalities, administered by the central government. Its strategic location on the Hàn River estuary, National Route 1, and the North–South Railway makes it a critical transport hub. The city has a high Human Development Index (0.800) and is a significant center for tourism, industry, and education in central Vietnam.

- **1535** — Portuguese explorer António de Faria is one of the first Europeans to visit.
- **1813** — Điện Hải Citadel is first built to protect the port.
- **1847** — French vessels bombard Đà Nẵng.

- **1 January 1997** — Da Nang is separated from Quảng Nam province to become a centrally controlled municipality.
- **2017** — Da Nang hosts the APEC summit.
- **1 July 2025** — A new, expanded Da Nang City is formed by incorporating neighboring Quảng Nam province.

## Body

### Names and Geography
Da Nang's name is likely derived from the Cham language, with proposed meanings including "opening of a large river" or "the large water." The city was historically known as Cửa Hàn ("mouth of the Hàn river") and as Tourane during French rule. Geographically, it is bordered by mountains to the west and the South China Sea to the east, with the Hàn River bisecting the central city. It has a tropical monsoon climate with a wet, typhoon-prone season from September to December and a dry season from January to August.

### History
The area's origins lie in the kingdom of Champa, with the Cham city of Indrapura located nearby until it was sacked by Đại Việt in 982 AD. Western contact began with the Portuguese in 1535. The French established colonial control after bombardments in 1847 and 1858, renaming the city Tourane in 1889. During the Vietnam War, Da Nang Air Base became one of the world's busiest aircraft hubs. After the war, the city developed as part of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and was established as a direct-controlled municipality in 1997.

### Demographics and Economy
Da Nang is the fifth-most populated city in Vietnam (1,269,070 people in 2024) with the country's highest urbanization ratio. The economy is diversified, with strengths in industry (machinery, textiles, shipbuilding), services, and tourism. The Da Nang Hi-Tech Park, established in 2010, focuses on sectors like biotechnology and IT. The city has led Vietnam's Provincial Competitiveness Index in multiple years, noted for infrastructure and transparency.

### Tourism and Culture
Tourism is a major economic driver, with attractions including Mỹ Khê and Non Nuoc beaches, the Ba Na Hills mountain resort, and the iconic Golden Bridge. The city is praised for its beaches and modern infrastructure, earning nicknames like "the Miami of Vietnam." Da Nang's cuisine features bold, spicy central Vietnamese flavors, with signature dishes like Mì Quảng (rice noodle dish) and Bánh tráng cuốn thịt heo (grilled rice paper with pork).

### Transportation
Da Nang is a key transport node. Its international airport is Vietnam's third-largest. The city is a station on the North–South Railway and is connected by National Highways 1 and 14B. The Hải Vân Tunnel (opened 2005) is Southeast Asia's longest road tunnel. The port system, the third largest in Vietnam, includes Tiên Sa Seaport and handles significant cargo and cruise ship traffic.

## Terms
- **Direct-controlled municipality**: A first-level administrative unit in Vietnam under the direct administration of the central government, not part of any province.
- **Champa**: A historical kingdom and civilization that existed in central and southern Vietnam from approximately the 2nd to the 17th century.
- **Đại Việt**: A historical Vietnamese kingdom, predecessor to modern Vietnam.
- **Provincial Competitiveness Index (PCI)**: An annual assessment in Vietnam that measures the quality of economic governance and business environment in provinces and cities.
- **Human Development Index (HDI)**: A composite statistic of life expectancy, education, and per capita income indicators, used to rank regions by level of human development.
- **East–West Economic Corridor (EWEC)**: An economic corridor connecting Vietnam, Laos, Thailand, and Myanmar.

## Debates and Open Questions
The exact etymology of the name "Da Nang" is debated among scholars, with multiple proposed origins from Chamic and other languages. The article also notes a major administrative change in 2025 where Da Nang incorporated Quảng Nam province, the long-term effects of which are yet to be seen.
