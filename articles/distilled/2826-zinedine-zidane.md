# Zinedine Zidane

## Overview
Zinedine Zidane is a French football icon, widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time, renowned for his elegance, vision, and technical mastery as an attacking midfielder. After a stellar playing career, he transitioned into management, achieving historic success with Real Madrid and becoming the head coach of the France national team. His career is marked by major trophies, individual accolades, and moments of both brilliance and controversy.

## Timeline
- **23 June 1972** — Born in Marseille, France.
- **18 May 1989** — Professional debut for Cannes.
- **1996** — Moves to Juventus, wins Serie A in first season.
- **1998** — Wins FIFA World Cup with France, scoring two goals in the final.
- **1998** — Wins first FIFA World Player of the Year and Ballon d'Or awards.
- **2001** — Transfers to Real Madrid for a then world-record fee of €77.5 million.
- **2002** — Scores iconic volley to win the UEFA Champions League for Real Madrid.
- **2006** — Scores in 2006 World Cup final; sent off for headbutt, then retires.
- **2016** — Appointed head coach of Real Madrid.
- **2016-2018** — Wins three consecutive UEFA Champions League titles as manager.
- **2026** — Appointed head coach of the France national team.

## Body

### Early Life and Playing Career
Born to Algerian parents in Marseille, Zidane began playing football in his neighbourhood before joining youth clubs. He turned professional with Cannes, where his talent was evident but so was a sensitivity to racial insults. He moved to Bordeaux, forming key partnerships, before joining Italian giants Juventus in 1996. At Juventus, he won two Serie A titles and reached two consecutive Champions League finals. In 2001, he joined Real Madrid for a world-record fee, where he scored a famous volley to win the 2002 Champions League and won La Liga in 2003.

### International Career with France
Zidane earned 108 caps for France. He became a national hero by scoring two headers in the 1998 World Cup final victory over Brazil. He then led France to win Euro 2000, where he was named Player of the Tournament. After a disappointing group-stage exit at the 2002 World Cup due to injury, he returned from international retirement to help France reach the 2006 World Cup final. There, he scored a penalty but was infamously sent off for headbutting Marco Materazzi, and France lost the shootout. He received the Golden Ball as the tournament's best player.

### Coaching Career
After retiring, Zidane began coaching at Real Madrid's reserve team. He was appointed first-team manager in January 2016. In his first two-and-a-half seasons, he won the Champions League three consecutive times (2016, 2017, 2018), a La Liga title, and other trophies, becoming the first coach to achieve the three-peat in the modern Champions League era. He resigned in 2018 but returned in 2019, winning another La Liga title before leaving again in 2021. In 2026, he was named head coach of the France national team.

### Legacy and Playing Style
Zidane is celebrated for his exceptional first touch, dribbling, passing, and ability to control games. He was a playmaker who could also score crucial goals, including in multiple finals. His career was also marked by occasional violent conduct, leading to 14 red cards. As a manager, he was praised for his man-management, tactical flexibility, and ability to unite star players, though some attributed part of his success to luck. He is one of the few individuals to have won the highest honours as both a player and a manager.

## Terms
- **Playmaker**: An attacking midfielder who controls the flow of a team's offensive play through vision and passing.
- **Ballon d'Or**: An annual football award presented by France Football to the best male player in the world.
- **FIFA World Player of the Year**: A former annual football award given by FIFA to the best male player in the world.
- **La Liga**: The top professional football division of the Spanish football league system.
- **Serie A**: The top professional football division of the Italian football league system.
- **UEFA Champions League**: An annual club football competition organized by UEFA, contested by top-division European clubs.
- **Galácticos**: A team policy of Real Madrid to sign world-famous star players.
- **Panenka**: A type of penalty kick where the player chips the ball gently down the center of the goal as the goalkeeper dives to one side.
- **Golden Ball**: An award given to the best player at a FIFA World Cup tournament.

## Debates and Open Questions
- **Temperament vs. Genius**: Zidane's career is often framed as a duality between his sublime talent and his volatile temperament, which led to crucial red cards, most notably in the 2006 World Cup final.
- **Coaching Credit**: Some debate whether his managerial success at Real Madrid was due to his tactical acumen or his exceptional man-management skills and the quality of his squad.
- **Cultural Identity**: As an Algerian-Frenchman, his identity and occasional reactions to racial provocation have been a point of discussion regarding his on-field conduct.
