# Cần Thơ

## Overview
Cần Thơ is a major municipality in Vietnam's Mekong Delta, known for its floating markets and riverine geography. It is the third-largest city in Vietnam and serves as a key economic, educational, and transport hub for the region. A significant administrative expansion in 2025 greatly increased its territory and population.

- **2024** — The population of Cần Thơ is recorded as approximately 4,199,824.

## Body

### Geography and Climate
Cần Thơ is located in the Mekong Delta, bordered by several provinces and the East Sea. Its landscape is defined by a complex network of rivers and canals, with the Hậu River being a vital waterway. The city has a tropical wet and dry climate (Aw) with two distinct seasons: a rainy season from May to November and a dry season from December to April. Average annual rainfall is 1,800 mm, with a mean temperature of 27 °C.

### Administrative Divisions
Following the 2025 reorganization, Cần Thơ municipality was expanded to include the former provinces of Hậu Giang and Sóc Trăng. It is now divided into 103 commune-level units, comprising 31 wards and 72 communes. Ninh Kiều, home to the Ninh Kiều port, is the former central district and remains the most populated area.

### Tourism and Economy
The city is a major tourist destination, famous for its floating markets, particularly the Cái Rang and Phong Điền markets, where commerce occurs on the river. Key attractions include the Ninh Kiều Wharf, the Trúc Lâm Phương Nam Zen Monastery, and the Cần Thơ Bridge. The region produces a variety of tropical fruits. The Cần Thơ City Museum details local history. Transport links include Cần Thơ International Airport, offering domestic flights, and connections via National Highway 1A from Ho Chi Minh City.

### Education and Media
Cần Thơ is an educational center for the Mekong Delta, hosting institutions such as Cần Thơ University, Cần Thơ University of Medicine and Pharmacy, and several other colleges. Vietnam Television operates a studio in the Ninh Kiều district, broadcasting regional news and programs on channels VTV Cần Thơ and VTV9.

## Terms
- **Municipality**: A type of administrative division in Vietnam, equivalent to a province-level city.
- **Ward (Phường)**: An urban-level administrative unit within a city or town.
- **Commune (Xã)**: A rural-level administrative unit.
- **Floating Market**: A traditional marketplace where goods are bought and sold from boats on a river or canal.
- **Mekong Delta**: A vast river delta in southwestern Vietnam formed by the distributaries of the Mekong River.
- **Köppen climate classification**: A system for classifying the world's climates based on temperature and precipitation patterns.
- **Resolution No. 202/2025/QH15**: The legal document passed by Vietnam's National Assembly to merge Hậu Giang and Sóc Trăng provinces into Cần Thơ.
