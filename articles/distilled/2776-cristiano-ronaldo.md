# Cristiano Ronaldo

## Overview
Cristiano Ronaldo is a Portuguese professional footballer, widely regarded as one of the greatest players in history. He is the all-time top goalscorer in professional football and holds numerous records for goals and appearances in both club and international football. His career, spanning over two decades, has been defined by prolific scoring, major trophy wins with multiple top clubs, and a sustained rivalry with Lionel Messi.

## Timeline
- **5 February 1985** — Born in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal.
- **12 August 2002** — Makes professional debut for Sporting CP.
- **12 August 2003** — Signs for Manchester United for £12 million.
- **21 May 2008** — Wins first Champions League with Manchester United; awarded first Ballon d'Or.
- **11 July 2009** — Transfers to Real Madrid for a then-world record £80 million.
- **24 May 2014** — Wins "La Décima" (10th Champions League) with Real Madrid.
- **10 July 2016** — Leads Portugal to victory at UEFA Euro 2016.
- **10 July 2018** — Transfers to Juventus for €100 million.
- **27 August 2021** — Returns to Manchester United.
- **30 December 2022** — Signs for Al-Nassr in Saudi Arabia.
- **23 June 2026** — Becomes first player to score in six FIFA World Cups.
- **21 May 2026** — Wins Saudi Pro League title with Al-Nassr.

## Body

### Early Life and Career Beginnings
Born into a poor family in Madeira, Ronaldo joined Sporting CP's youth system at age 12. He was diagnosed with tachycardia (a heart condition causing a rapid heartbeat) at age 15, which required cardiac ablation surgery. He left school at 14 to focus on football. His performance in a 2003 friendly for Sporting against Manchester United led to his record-breaking transfer to the English club.

### Manchester United (2003–2009)
Ronaldo developed from a winger into a prolific goalscorer at Manchester United. He won three consecutive Premier League titles (2007–09) and the 2007–08 Champions League. His 42 goals in the 2007–08 season earned him his first Ballon d'Or and the FIFA World Player of the Year award.

### Real Madrid (2009–2018)
At Real Madrid, Ronaldo became the club's all-time top goalscorer and the Champions League's all-time top scorer. He won four Champions League titles (2014, 2016, 2017, 2018), including the club's long-awaited "La Décima" in 2014. He won four more Ballon d'Or awards (2013, 2014, 2016, 2017) during this period.

### Juventus (2018–2021)
Ronaldo won two consecutive Serie A titles (2019, 2020) and the Coppa Italia (2021) with Juventus. He became the first player to win league titles in England, Spain, and Italy. In 2021, he finished as Serie A's top scorer (Capocannoniere).

### Later Career (2021–Present)
After a brief return to Manchester United, Ronaldo joined Al-Nassr in 2023. He led them to the Saudi Pro League title in 2026 and became the league's top scorer in 2024 and 2025. He is the first player to score 100 goals for four different clubs.

### International Career
Ronaldo debuted for Portugal in 2003 and became the country's all-time top scorer and most-capped player. He captained Portugal to their first major title at Euro 2016 and won the UEFA Nations League in 2019 and 2025. He holds the records for most international goals (146) and appearances (233). In 2026, he became the first player to score in six World Cup tournaments.

### Playing Style and Legacy
Ronaldo evolved from a skillful winger into a complete forward, renowned for his heading ability, powerful shooting, and penalty-taking. He is noted for his exceptional work ethic and physical conditioning. His career is often compared to that of Lionel Messi, with whom he shares a long-standing rivalry for the title of the best player of his generation.

## Terms
- **Ballon d'Or**: An annual football award presented by *France Football* to the best male player in the world.
- **Champions League**: Europe's premier club football competition, organized by UEFA.
- **Capocannoniere**: The award for the top goalscorer in Italy's Serie A.
- **Pichichi Trophy**: The award for the top goalscorer in Spain's La Liga.
- **Cardiac ablation**: A medical procedure to treat heart rhythm problems by scarring or destroying tissue in the heart.
- **Tachycardia**: A condition in which the heart beats too fast.
- **False winger**: A tactical role where a player starts on the wing but drifts centrally to act as an additional striker.

## Debates and Open Questions
The primary scholarly and fan debate concerns Ronaldo's place in football history, specifically in comparison to Lionel Messi. The discussion often centers on contrasting their playing styles, personalities, and trophy cabinets. While Ronaldo is celebrated for his athleticism, goal-scoring records across multiple leagues, and leadership, Messi is often praised for his playmaking and dribbling. The debate is subjective and remains unresolved, with both players widely considered among the greatest of all time.
