# Yuzuru Hanyu

## Overview
Yuzuru Hanyu is a Japanese figure skater and ice show producer, widely regarded as one of the greatest in history for his technical skill, artistic range, and longevity. He is the first man since Dick Button to win back-to-back Olympic titles (2014, 2018) and the first Asian Olympic champion in men's singles. His career, marked by numerous world records and innovations, profoundly impacted the sport's technical and artistic standards. After turning professional in 2022, he pioneered a new genre of narrative-driven solo ice shows.

## Timeline
- **Dec 7, 1994** — Born in Sendai, Japan.
- **2004** — Begins competitive skating career.
- **Mar 11, 2011** — Experiences the Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami in Sendai.
- **2012** — Wins first World Championship bronze medal; moves to train in Toronto.
- **Feb 2014** — Wins Olympic gold in Sochi, becoming the first Asian men's singles champion.
- **2016** — Lands the first quadruple loop jump in international competition.
- **Feb 2018** — Wins second Olympic gold in Pyeongchang, becoming the first man in 66 years to defend the title.
- **Feb 2020** — Wins Four Continents Championships to complete the Super Slam.
- **Jul 19, 2022** — Announces retirement from competitive skating to turn professional.
- **Feb 26, 2023** — Performs *Gift* at Tokyo Dome, the first ice show held there.

## Body

### Competitive Career and Achievements
Hanyu's competitive career (2004–2022) was defined by historic firsts and record-breaking performances. He is the first singles skater to win four consecutive Grand Prix Finals (2013–2016) and the only male besides Jan Hoffmann to win seven World Championship medals in the post-war era. He broke world records 19 times, the most in singles under the ISU Judging System introduced in 2003. A key technical pioneer, he was the first to land a quadruple loop in international competition and the first to attempt a quadruple Axel at the Olympics (2022). His rivalry with skaters like Patrick Chan and Nathan Chen defined eras of the sport.

### Technical Skill and Artistry
Analysts describe Hanyu as a complete skater, combining exceptional technical elements with mature artistry. He is noted for generating speed with minimal strokes, allowing for complex jump entries like his signature triple Axel from a spread eagle. His jumps are characterized by impressive height and distance, with his triple Axel at the 2019 Worlds measuring 70 cm high and 3.62 m long. He executed four types of quadruple jumps and difficult spins like the layback Biellmann. His programs spanned diverse music genres, and he was deeply involved in their creation, from music selection to costume design.

### Professional Career and Ice Story Series
After turning professional in 2022, Hanyu launched the *Yuzuru Hanyu Ice Story* series, creating a new genre of narrative ice shows. The prelude *Prologue* (2022) was the first solo ice show production. *Gift* (2023) at Tokyo Dome set an audience record of 35,000. The *Repray Tour* (2023–24) was the first solo tour in figure skating. These shows weave high-level skating into philosophical stories with elaborate narration, attracting over 100,000 spectators by February 2024. He also chairs the annual *Notte Stellata* charity show commemorating the 2011 earthquake.

### Philanthropy and Public Life
The 2011 Tōhoku earthquake profoundly shaped Hanyu's life. He has donated over $3 million for reconstruction and disaster prevention, including his full Olympic prize money and autobiography royalties. He serves as a Tsunami Disaster Prevention Ambassador and has organized charity exhibitions and events. Hanyu is a highly marketable figure, endorsing brands like ANA and Gucci. He graduated from Waseda University in 2020 with a thesis on using 3D motion capture for figure skating judging.

## Terms
- **Super Slam**: Winning all major ISU championship titles in a discipline at both junior and senior levels. Hanyu is the first men's singles skater to achieve this.
- **Quadruple Axel**: A jump with four and a half revolutions. Hanyu attempted it at the 2022 Olympics; it was first successfully landed in competition by Ilia Malinin in 2022.
- **ISU Judging System**: The scoring system used in figure skating since 2003, which replaced the 6.0 system. Hanyu broke 19 world records under it.
- **Grade of Execution (GOE)**: A score component that evaluates the quality of a technical element, ranging from -5 to +5.
- **Triple Axel**: A jump with three and a half revolutions, considered Hanyu's signature element.
- **Ice Story**: Hanyu's professional solo show series, which combines athletic skating with narrative storytelling.

## Debates and Open Questions
- **Jumps vs. Artistry**: Hanyu argued that technical jumps form the foundation for artistry, stating that a "strong foundation in technique" is necessary for "full artistry." This addresses the ongoing debate in the sport about the balance between technical difficulty and artistic expression.
- **Quadruple Axel Feasibility**: Hanyu's pursuit of the quadruple Axel, a jump not yet landed in competition during his competitive career, sparked debate about its technical possibility and its role in the sport's future.
- **Legacy and Impact**: While widely considered one of the greatest, the exact ranking of Hanyu among legends like Dick Button or Evgeni Plushenko is subjective and debated among fans and experts.
