# Lionel Messi

## Overview
Lionel Messi is an Argentine professional footballer widely regarded as one of the greatest players in history. His career is defined by record-breaking individual achievements, including eight Ballons d'Or, and a trophy-laden journey with Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain, Inter Miami, and the Argentina national team, culminating in a 2022 FIFA World Cup victory.

## Body

### Early Life and Youth Career
Lionel Messi was born in Rosario, Argentina, into a football-loving family. At age 10, he was diagnosed with a growth hormone deficiency, requiring costly therapy. After Newell's Old Boys reneged on financial support, Messi's family arranged a trial with FC Barcelona. In December 2000, club director Carles Rexach famously agreed to sign him on a paper napkin. Messi moved to Spain in 2001, initially struggling with homesickness and integration at the La Masia academy. He progressed rapidly through Barcelona's youth teams, becoming a key member of the famed "Baby Dream Team."

### Club Career: Barcelona (2004–2021)
Messi made his first-team debut in 2004 and quickly became integral. Under manager Pep Guardiola (2008–2012), he became the tactical focal point, winning four consecutive Ballons d'Or and helping Barcelona achieve a historic treble in 2008–09. He set numerous records, including most goals in a calendar year (91 in 2012) and becoming Barcelona's all-time top scorer. The 2014–15 season saw the formation of the prolific "MSN" attack (Messi, Suárez, Neymar), leading to a second continental treble. Messi was named club captain in 2018. Despite his success, financial difficulties at Barcelona forced his departure in 2021.

### Club Career: PSG and Inter Miami (2021–Present)
Messi joined Paris Saint-Germain in August 2021, winning two Ligue 1 titles. In July 2023, he moved to Inter Miami in Major League Soccer (MLS), a transfer that significantly raised the league's profile. He led Miami to its first trophy (Leagues Cup 2023) and its first MLS Cup championship in 2025, winning back-to-back league MVP awards. In March 2026, he scored his 900th career goal.

### International Career
Messi debuted for Argentina in 2005. After winning Olympic gold in 2008, he became captain in 2011 and endured several final defeats, including the 2014 World Cup. He briefly retired from international football in 2016 but returned. The 2020s marked his greatest international success: he led Argentina to victory in the 2021 Copa América, the 2022 FIFA World Cup (where he won a second Golden Ball), and the 2024 Copa América. At the 2026 World Cup, he led Argentina to a second consecutive final, winning the Silver Boot and Silver Ball.

### Player Profile and Reception
Messi is a left-footed forward known for his low centre of gravity, exceptional dribbling, vision, and goalscoring. He evolved from a winger to a false nine and later a deep-lying playmaker. He is widely considered one of the two best players of his generation alongside Cristiano Ronaldo, with whom he has a storied rivalry. He is also frequently compared to Argentine legend Diego Maradona, a comparison solidified by his 2022 World Cup triumph.

## Terms
- **Ballon d'Or**: An annual football award presented by France Football to the best male player in the world.
- **Treble**: Winning a domestic league, primary domestic cup, and the main continental club competition in a single season.
- **False Nine**: A tactical role where a centre-forward drops deep into midfield, drawing defenders and creating space.
- **Pichichi Trophy**: An award given to the top goalscorer in Spain's La Liga.
- **European Golden Shoe**: An award for the leading goalscorer in league matches from the top division of every European national league.
- **CONMEBOL**: The South American Football Confederation, governing football in South America.
- **La Masia**: FC Barcelona's youth academy.
- **Messidependencia**: A term describing Barcelona's tactical and psychological dependence on Messi.

## Debates and Open Questions
- **Rivalry with Cristiano Ronaldo**: A long-standing debate among fans and pundits comparing the merits, achievements, and playing styles of Messi and Ronaldo to determine the superior player.
- **Comparison with Diego Maradona**: Ongoing discussion about whether Messi, especially after his 2022 World Cup win, has surpassed Maradona's legacy and standing in Argentine society.
- **Leadership and Temperament**: Early in his international career, questions were raised about Messi's leadership and passion for the national team, a perception that shifted significantly after his later successes.
