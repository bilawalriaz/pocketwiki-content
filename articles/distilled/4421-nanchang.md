# Nanchang

## Overview
Nanchang is the capital of Jiangxi Province in southeastern China, strategically located on the Gan River near Poyang Lake. Its historical significance stems from its role as an administrative center since the Han dynasty and as the site of the 1927 Nanchang Uprising, which the Communist Party recognizes as the birth of the People's Liberation Army. Today, it is a major industrial, transportation, and educational hub.

## Body

### Imperial and Early History
The area was first incorporated into China during the Qin dynasty as Jiujiang Commandery. In 201 BC, the city was named Nanchang, meaning "southern flourishing," and became an administrative seat. It grew in importance, becoming the provincial center of Jiangxi by 763. The construction of the Tengwang Pavilion in 653 and its celebration in a famous 675 poem cemented the city's cultural fame. Throughout subsequent dynasties, it served as a regional capital and was a site of conflict, including a rebellion in the early 16th century and battles at the end of the Yuan dynasty. Its commercial importance declined in the late 19th century due to shifting trade routes.

### Modern History and the Nanchang Uprising
On August 1, 1927, the Nanchang Uprising, led by pro-communist officers, provided the foundational troops and organization for the future People's Liberation Army (PLA). The city was later the site of a major battle in 1939 during the Second Sino-Japanese War, resulting in over 80,000 Chinese casualties and more than 20,000 Japanese casualties. After 1949, Nanchang was extensively industrialized, developing major industries in textiles, paper, machinery, automotive production, and chemicals.

### Geography and Climate
Nanchang is located 130 km south of the Yangtze River on the Gan River. It has a humid subtropical climate with four distinct seasons. Summers are long and humid with high temperatures, while winters are short and cool. The city receives significant rainfall, particularly from April to June.

### Economy and Transportation
Nanchang is a regional agricultural hub and a center for industries including aircraft manufacturing, automobiles, metallurgy, and pharmaceuticals. Its GDP was 500.319 billion yuan in 2017. The city is a major transportation hub with converging high-speed railways, an international airport (Changbei), extensive national highways, and a metro system opened in 2015. Water routes on the Gan River are also economically important.

## Terms
- **Commandery (Jun):** An administrative division in ancient China, similar to a province or prefecture.
- **Prefecture (Zhou):** A historical administrative division in China, often larger than a county.
- **Nanchang Uprising:** The 1927 armed insurrection led by communists that is considered the founding event of the People's Liberation Army.
- **People's Liberation Army (PLA):** The military arm of the Chinese Communist Party.
- **Tengwang Pavilion:** A historic tower in Nanchang, first built in 653, celebrated in classical literature.
- **Gan River:** The major river flowing through Nanchang and Jiangxi Province.
- **Poyang Lake:** China's largest freshwater lake, located near Nanchang.
- **Humid Subtropical Climate (Köppen Cfa):** A climate characterized by hot, humid summers and mild, drier winters.
- **Gross Regional Product (GRP):** The total value of goods and services produced in a region, equivalent to GDP for a city or province.
