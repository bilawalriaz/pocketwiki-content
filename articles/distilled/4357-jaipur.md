# Jaipur

## Overview
Jaipur is the capital and largest city of Rajasthan, India. Founded in 1727, it is one of the earliest planned cities of modern India, renowned for its distinctive pink architecture and as a major tourist destination forming part of the Golden Triangle.

## Timeline
- **1727** — Founded by Sawai Jai Singh II, shifting the capital from Amer.
- **1876** — City painted pink to welcome the Prince of Wales, giving it the "Pink City" epithet.
- **1949** — Became the capital of the newly formed state of Rajasthan.
- **2005** — Jaipur Airport granted international status.
- **2015** — Jaipur Metro commenced commercial operation.
- **2019** — Named to the UNESCO World Heritage Cities list.

## Body

### Foundation and Planning
Jaipur was founded in 1727 by Sawai Jai Singh II, the Kachhwaha Rajput ruler of Amer, to address population growth and water scarcity in his old capital. The city was designed by architect Vidyadhar Bhattacharya based on principles of Vastu Shastra and Shilpa Shastra. Its layout is notable for its regularity, with broad, gridded streets dividing the city into nine blocks. The city's distinctive pink color dates to 1876, when it was painted to welcome Albert Edward, Prince of Wales.

### Geography and Climate
Jaipur covers 467 square kilometres in northeastern Rajasthan, surrounded by the Aravalli range. It has a hot semi-arid climate with extremely hot summers and mild winters. The Dravyavati River is the primary drainage channel, which underwent a rejuvenation project completed in 2022.

### Demographics and Administration
As of the 2011 census, Jaipur had a population of about 3.05 million. Hindi is the official language, with Dhundari as the native dialect. The city is administered by the Jaipur Development Authority and two municipal corporations (Greater Jaipur and Jaipur Heritage) established in 2020.

### Economy
Jaipur's economy is driven by tourism, gemstone cutting, jewellery manufacturing, textiles, and IT. It is a major hub for arts and crafts, including traditional textiles and the production of the "Jaipur foot" prosthetic. The city's nominal GDP for 2020–21 was estimated at ₹1,22,140 crores.

### Tourism and Culture
Jaipur is a key part of India's Golden Triangle tourist circuit. Major attractions include Amer Fort and Jantar Mantar (both UNESCO World Heritage Sites), Hawa Mahal, and the City Palace. The city is famous for its traditional crafts, performing arts like the Jaipur Gharana of Kathak dance, and festivals such as the Jaipur Literature Festival.

### Infrastructure and Transport
Jaipur is well-connected by air (Jaipur International Airport), rail (Jaipur Junction, the busiest station in Rajasthan), and road (on National Highway 48). The Jaipur Metro, India's sixth metro system, began operations in 2015.

## Terms
- **Vastu Shastra**: An ancient Indian system of architecture and design principles.
- **Shilpa Shastra**: Ancient Indian texts describing arts, crafts, and their design rules.
- **Golden Triangle**: A popular tourist circuit in India connecting Delhi, Agra, and Jaipur.
- **Kachhwaha**: The Rajput clan to which Jaipur's founder, Jai Singh II, belonged.
- **Jaipur Gharana**: One of the three major schools (gharanas) of the North Indian classical dance form Kathak.
- **Jaipur foot**: A rubber-based prosthetic leg designed and produced in Jaipur for below-knee amputations.
