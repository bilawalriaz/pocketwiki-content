# Ankara

## Overview
Ankara is the capital city of Turkey, located in central Anatolia. It is Turkey's second-largest city by population after Istanbul. Historically known as Ancyra and Angora, the city has been a significant settlement for millennia, serving as the capital of the ancient Celtic state of Galatia, a major Roman provincial capital, and the headquarters of the Turkish National Movement. It became the capital of the Republic of Turkey in 1923, succeeding the Ottoman capital Istanbul.

## Body

### Ancient and Classical History
The region's history traces back to the Bronze Age Hattic civilization, succeeded by Hittites, Phrygians, Lydians, Persians, and Greeks. The city, then called Ancyra (Greek for "anchor"), expanded as a trading center under the Greeks of Pontos around 300 BC. In 278 BC, Celtic Galatians made it a tribal capital. It passed to Roman control in 25 BC, when Emperor Augustus made it the capital of Galatia. Roman Ancyra was a major administrative and trading hub, strategically located on key roads. Its importance is evidenced by the Monumentum Ancyranum, which preserves the Res Gestae Divi Augusti.

### Byzantine, Seljuk, and Ottoman Periods
Under Byzantine rule, Ancyra was a center of Christian activity, hosting several church synods. It was captured by Arabs in 654 and later by Seljuk Turks in 1073. After periods of control by Crusaders and Danishmends, it became part of the Seljuk Sultanate of Rum. Following the Mongol victory at Köse Dağ in 1243, the city was taken by the Ottoman Empire in 1356, though Timur's forces captured it in 1402 before it returned to Ottoman control. It served as the capital of various Ottoman administrative divisions (eyalets and vilayets) until the empire's fall.

### Turkish Republican Capital
After Ottoman defeat in World War I, Mustafa Kemal Atatürk established his resistance headquarters in Angora in 1920. Following the Turkish War of Independence, the Republic of Turkey was proclaimed on 29 October 1923, with Angora (officially renamed Ankara) as its new capital. The city experienced phenomenal growth from a small town of about 35,000 in 1924 to a major metropolis. It is divided into the historic Ulus district and the modern Yenişehir, centered on Kızılay Square.

### Geography and Climate
Ankara is located between the Kızılırmak and Sakarya rivers in the Central Anatolia Region. It has a cold semi-arid climate with cold, snowy winters and hot, dry summers. The annual average precipitation is 414 mm (16 in).

### Economy and Infrastructure
Ankara is a major commercial and industrial city, home to Turkey's defense and aerospace industries (e.g., Turkish Aerospace Industries, ASELSAN). The government is a prominent employer. Historically, the region exported mohair, Angora wool, and agricultural products like pears and honey. The city is a central hub for Turkey's road and railway networks, including high-speed rail (YHT) lines, and is served by Esenboğa Airport.

### Culture and Sights
Ankara's main sights include the Ankara Castle, the ancient Temple of Augustus and Rome (Monumentum Ancyranum), Roman Baths, and the Anıtkabir mausoleum of Mustafa Kemal Atatürk. The city houses the Museum of Anatolian Civilizations and numerous mosques, such as the Kocatepe Mosque and the historic Alâeddin Mosque. It is a center for the arts, hosting the Turkish State Opera and Ballet and several orchestras and festivals.

## Terms
*   **Ancyra**: The ancient Greek and Latin name for Ankara, meaning "anchor."
*   **Galatia**: An ancient Celtic state and later a Roman province in central Anatolia, with Ancyra as its capital.
*   **Res Gestae Divi Augusti**: An inscription recording the achievements of Emperor Augustus, preserved on the walls of the Temple of Augustus and Rome in Ankara.
*   **Seljuk Sultanate of Rum**: A medieval Turkic state that ruled much of Anatolia after the Battle of Manzikert (1071).
*   **Eyalet/Vilayet**: Administrative divisions of the Ottoman Empire.
*   **Grand National Assembly of Turkey**: The parliament established in Ankara in 1920, which became the legislative body of the new republic.
*   **Anıtkabir**: The mausoleum of Mustafa Kemal Atatürk, the founder of the Republic of Turkey.
*   **Gecekondu**: Informally built housing that appeared rapidly in Ankara due to rural migration after the 1950s.
*   **Angora**: The former English name for Ankara, preserved in the names of Angora cats, goats, and rabbits.

## Debates and Open Questions
*   The identification of Ankara with the Hittite cult center **Ankuwaš** is a matter of debate.
*   The exact date of the city's capture by the Seljuk Sultanate of Rum after 1073 is unknown.
*   There is debate over whether Atatürk's famous quote at the Security Monument is "Be proud" (*övün*) or "Use your mind" (*öğün*).
