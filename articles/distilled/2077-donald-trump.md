# Donald Trump

## Overview
Donald John Trump (born June 14, 1946) is an American politician, media personality, and businessman who has served as the 45th (2017–2021) and 47th (2025–present) president of the United States. A Republican, his political movement, Trumpism, has reshaped the party. His presidencies are marked by significant policy shifts, legal controversies, and a leadership style scholars often characterize as authoritarian.

## Timeline
- **June 14, 1946** — Born in Queens, New York City.
- **1968** — Graduates from the University of Pennsylvania with an economics degree.
- **1971** — Becomes president of his family's real estate company, later renamed the Trump Organization.
- **2004–2015** — Hosts the reality TV show *The Apprentice*.
- **November 8, 2016** — Wins the presidential election against Hillary Clinton.
- **January 20, 2017** — Inaugurated as the 45th president.
- **December 18, 2019** — First impeachment by the House (acquitted by the Senate in Feb. 2020).
- **November 3, 2020** — Loses the presidential election to Joe Biden.
- **January 6, 2021** — A mob of his supporters attacks the U.S. Capitol.
- **January 13, 2021** — Second impeachment by the House (acquitted by the Senate in Feb. 2021).
- **May 30, 2024** — Found guilty on 34 felony counts in a New York state court.
- **November 5, 2024** — Wins the presidential election against Kamala Harris.
- **January 20, 2025** — Inaugurated as the 47th president.
- **February 2026** — U.S. Supreme Court rules some of his imposed tariffs illegal.

## Body

### Early Life and Business Career
Born into a wealthy New York family, Trump graduated from the University of Pennsylvania in 1968. He took over his father's real estate business in 1971, renaming it the Trump Organization. His early career was shaped by mentor Roy Cohn. Trump expanded into Manhattan with projects like the Grand Hyatt Hotel and Trump Tower. His ventures included Atlantic City casinos, golf courses, and licensing the Trump name. His businesses filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy six times between 1991 and 2009. He also launched ventures like the Trump Shuttle airline and the Miss Universe pageants.

### First Presidency (2017–2021)
Trump's first term followed an "America First" agenda. Domestically, he signed the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, rolled back environmental regulations, and appointed three Supreme Court justices. Immigration policies included a travel ban on several Muslim-majority countries, border wall expansion, and a family separation policy. He withdrew the U.S. from the Paris Agreement and initiated a trade war with China. His administration's COVID-19 response was widely criticized. After losing the 2020 election, he refused to concede, attempting to overturn results, culminating in the January 6, 2021, Capitol attack. He was impeached twice but acquitted by the Senate both times.

### Legal Issues and Second Presidency
Between terms, Trump faced numerous legal challenges, including liability for sexual abuse, defamation, and business fraud. In May 2024, he became the first former president convicted of a felony for falsifying business records. After winning the 2024 election, two federal felony indictments were dismissed due to Justice Department policy against prosecuting a sitting president.

Trump began his second term in 2025 with mass federal layoffs, high tariffs, and signing the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. His administration has targeted political opponents, restricted transgender rights, pursued mass deportations, and used executive orders extensively, drawing over 550 lawsuits. Foreign policy includes an expansionist agenda, military actions in Latin America, and authorizing strikes on Iran that began the 2026 Iran war.

### Rhetoric and Political Style
Trump's political style, Trumpism, is described as right-wing populist. His rhetoric is widely characterized as racist, misogynistic, and fearmongering. He has made a record number of false or misleading statements and promoted conspiracy theories, including the "birther" theory and false claims about the 2020 election. Researchers describe his actions as authoritarian and contributing to democratic backsliding. He frequently labels critical news media as "fake news."

## Terms
- **Trumpism**: A political movement characterized by right-wing populism, nationalism, and expanded presidential power.
- **Chapter 11 bankruptcy**: A form of bankruptcy protection allowing a business to reorganize debts and continue operating.
- **Unitary executive theory**: A legal theory arguing the president has complete control over the executive branch, limiting congressional and judicial power.
- **Stochastic terrorism**: The use of mass media to provoke random acts of ideologically motivated violence, statistically predictable but individually unpredictable.
- **Firehosing**: A propaganda technique overwhelming an audience with many messages, including false ones, to create doubt and confusion.
- **Consent decree**: A settlement between parties in a lawsuit, often court-approved, to resolve a dispute without an admission of guilt.
- **Blind trust**: A financial arrangement where a public official places assets under an independent trustee's control to avoid conflicts of interest.
- **Alien Enemies Act of 1798**: A federal law allowing the president to detain or deport non-citizens from a hostile nation during wartime.

## Debates and Open Questions
- **Authoritarianism and Democratic Backsliding**: Scholars debate whether Trump's actions—targeting opponents, challenging judicial authority, using executive orders to bypass Congress—represent a shift toward authoritarianism and declining democratic norms.
- **Impact on the Republican Party**: Analysis continues on how Trump has reshaped the party's identity, policies, and voter base, and whether this represents a permanent realignment.
- **Economic Policies**: Economists debate the long-term effects of his protectionist trade policies, large tax cuts, and high tariffs on the U.S. and global economies.
- **Legal Legacy**: The implications of his multiple impeachments, criminal conviction, and indictment dismissals due to presidential immunity set new precedents for presidential accountability.
- **Foreign Policy Shifts**: Analysts question the sustainability and consequences of his transactional "America First" foreign policy, including its impact on alliances and global stability.
