# Heinrich Himmler

## Overview
Heinrich Himmler was a leading Nazi politician and the longest-serving Reichsführer-SS (1929–1945). As a principal architect of the Holocaust, he was responsible for the genocide of millions and the operation of concentration and extermination camps. His power grew through the SS, which he transformed from a small bodyguard unit into one of Nazi Germany's most powerful institutions.

## Timeline
- **7 October 1900** — Born in Munich, Germany.
- **1 August 1923** — Joins the Nazi Party.
- **1925** — Joins the SS (Schutzstaffel).
- **January 1929** — Becomes Reichsführer-SS.
- **30 January 1933** — Hitler appointed Chancellor; Nazi Party comes to power.
- **20 July 1934** — SS becomes an independent organization after the Night of the Long Knives.
- **17 June 1936** — Named Chief of German Police.
- **20 January 1942** — Attends the Wannsee Conference, which outlines the "Final Solution."
- **4 October 1943** — Delivers the Posen speech, explicitly referencing the extermination of Jews.
- **20 July 1944** — After the failed plot to assassinate Hitler, Himmler is given command of the Replacement Army.
- **28 April 1945** — Dismissed by Hitler after secret peace negotiations are revealed.
- **23 May 1945** — Commits suicide in British custody after capture.

## Body

### Rise in the SS
Himmler joined the Nazi Party in 1923 and the SS in 1925. He became Reichsführer-SS in 1929, growing the force from 290 men to over 52,000 by 1933. He established key SS departments, including the SD (Security Service) under Reinhard Heydrich, and the Race and Settlement Main Office (RuSHA) to enforce racial purity. In 1933, he set up the first official concentration camp at Dachau, which became a model for the camp system. By 1936, he was Chief of German Police, unifying all police forces under SS control.

### World War II and the Holocaust
Himmler was a central figure in Nazi racial policy and the Holocaust. He authorized the Einsatzgruppen death squads, which murdered tens of thousands in Poland and the Soviet Union. He commissioned Generalplan Ost, a plan for the ethnic cleansing and colonization of Eastern Europe that resulted in millions of deaths. Following the Wannsee Conference in 1942, he oversaw the "Final Solution," the systematic extermination of European Jews. He expanded the camp system, including Auschwitz, and ordered the construction of extermination camps like Treblinka. In secret 1943 speeches at Posen, he explicitly referred to the "extermination of the Jewish people."

### Germanization and Mysticism
Himmler was deeply involved in Germanization policies in the East, implementing Generalplan Ost to resettle ethnic Germans and eliminate or enslave Slavic populations. He ordered the kidnapping of children deemed "racially valuable" from Eastern Europe. Himmler promoted a cult of ancestor worship and Norse mysticism within the SS, founding the pseudoscientific Ahnenerbe society to search for proof of Aryan superiority. He sought to replace Christianity with a new SS moral code.

### Final Years and Death
In the war's final years, Himmler held high military commands, including Army Group Vistula, but failed to achieve his objectives. In March 1945, he attempted unauthorized peace negotiations with the Western Allies. When Hitler learned of this, he dismissed Himmler from all posts and branded him a traitor. Himmler attempted to flee in disguise but was captured by British forces. He committed suicide by biting a cyanide capsule on 23 May 1945.

## Terms
- **Reichsführer-SS**: The highest rank and title of the commander of the SS.
- **Schutzstaffel (SS)**: Originally Hitler's bodyguard unit, it grew under Himmler into a powerful paramilitary organization responsible for security, intelligence, and racial policy.
- **Einsatzgruppen**: Mobile killing squads of SS and police personnel that followed the German army into occupied territories, murdering Jews, communists, and others.
- **Generalplan Ost**: The Nazi German government's plan for the genocide, ethnic cleansing, and colonization of Central and Eastern Europe.
- **Wannsee Conference**: A January 1942 meeting of senior Nazi officials to coordinate the implementation of the "Final Solution to the Jewish Question."
- **Ahnenerbe**: A pseudoscientific institute founded by Himmler to research the supposed ancestral heritage of the "Aryan race."
- **Waffen-SS**: The combat branch of the SS, which served alongside the regular German army (Wehrmacht) in World War II.
- **RSHA (Reich Security Main Office)**: The central SS office that combined the SD, Gestapo, and Kripo, overseeing intelligence and security in Nazi Germany.

## Debates and Open Questions
- Historians debate the exact nature of Himmler's relationship with Hitler, noting his subservience yet ambition to succeed him.
- The precise motivations behind Himmler's failed peace negotiations in 1945 and whether he acted out of ideology, self-preservation, or a mix of both remain subjects of analysis.
- The full extent of his personal involvement in specific operational decisions of the Holocaust, versus his role as an administrator and enforcer of Hitler's will, is continually studied.
