# Musician

## Overview
A musician is a professional who composes, conducts, or performs music. The term encompasses a wide range of roles, from songwriters and conductors to instrumentalists and singers, whose work is shaped by culture, skill, and creative preference. Understanding the different types of musicians is key to appreciating the collaborative and diverse nature of musical creation and performance.

## Body

### Core Definition and Roles
A musician is broadly defined as someone who composes, conducts, or performs music. This professional designation includes songwriters (who write music and lyrics), conductors (who direct performances), and performers (who play instruments or sing). Musicians can work alone or in groups like bands or orchestras, often specializing in a genre but frequently blending styles.

### Types of Musicians
The source categorizes musicians into several primary types. A **composer** creates musical compositions, a title mainly used for those writing classical or film music. A **conductor** directs a musical performance, using gestures to lead an ensemble like an orchestra or choir. A **performer** is either an instrumentalist or a singer who performs for an audience, either solo or as part of an ensemble. A **music producer**, while not always a musician, often is one and plays a crucial role in shaping the sound of a recording and overseeing its production.

### Influences and Output
A musician's output is influenced by factors including culture, skill set, life experience, education, and creative preferences. A musician who records and releases music is often called a recording artist. Those who primarily write and record art music, media soundtracks, scores for film, theatre, or video games, or classical music are usually called composers.

## Terms
- **Musician**: A general term for a person who follows music as a profession, including composers, conductors, and performers.
- **Composer**: A musician who creates musical compositions, principally for classical or film music.
- **Conductor**: A musician who directs a musical performance, typically an orchestra or choral group, using gestures.
- **Performer**: A musician who plays a musical instrument (instrumentalist) or sings (vocalist) for an audience.
- **Songwriter**: A musician who writes both music and lyrics for songs.
- **Lyricist**: A person who mainly writes the words for songs.
- **Recording Artist**: A musician who records and releases music.
- **Music Producer**: A professional who oversees the recording process and shapes the sound of a song or album; may also be a musician.
