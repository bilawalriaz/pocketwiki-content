# Henan

## Overview
Henan is a central Chinese province historically known as the Central Plains (Zhongyuan), considered the core region of Chinese cultural history. It is China's third-most populous province and its largest inland provincial economy, serving as a major agricultural, industrial, and transportation hub. Its strategic location has made it a cradle of civilization but also a frequent site of conflict and natural disasters, particularly floods from the Yellow River.

## Timeline
- **c. 1600 – c. 1046 BC** — Shang dynasty capitals centered in Henan; oracle bones discovered at Yinxu (Anyang).
- **11th century BC** — Zhou people overthrow the Shang dynasty at the Battle of Muye.
- **221 BC** — Qin Shi Huang unifies China; Han dynasty later makes Luoyang its capital.
- **982** — Song dynasty reunifies China with its capital at Kaifeng, which becomes the world's largest city.
- **1127** — Jurchens capture Kaifeng, ending the Northern Song dynasty.
- **1368** — Ming court organizes resettlement from Shanxi to Henan; northern prefectures placed under Henan's government.
- **1642** — Ming troops breach Yellow River dikes to flood rebel-held Kaifeng, killing hundreds of thousands.
- **1887** — Major Yellow River dike breach at Zhengzhou causes a flood killing over two million people.
- **1938** — Nationalist government bombs Huayuankou dam to halt Japanese advance, causing massive flooding.
- **1954** — Provincial capital moved from Kaifeng to Zhengzhou due to railway importance.
- **1958** — Yashan in Suiping County becomes China's first people's commune, beginning the Great Leap Forward.
- **1975** — Banqiao Dam collapse from Typhoon Nina rainfall kills at least 26,000 people.

## Body

### Historical Significance and Turmoil
Henan's fertile plains and central location made it the heartland of ancient Chinese cultures (Yangshao, Longshan) and the first dynasties (Xia, Shang, Zhou). It hosted multiple historical capitals, including Luoyang, Anyang, Kaifeng, and Zhengzhou. This strategic importance also brought repeated devastation from warfare and the Yellow River's floods. The river's course changed significantly in 1194, and catastrophic floods occurred in 1642, 1887, 1938, and 1975, causing immense loss of life and displacement. The region suffered greatly during the late Ming/Qing wars, the Great Leap Forward famines of the 1960s, and the 1942-43 famine.

### Demographics and Society
With a population of over 98 million (2020 census), Henan is the world's fifth most populous sub-national division. It is 98.8% Han Chinese and exhibits a late-stage demographic transition with a declining birth rate and an aging population. The province has a historically unbalanced gender ratio due to the one-child policy, though it has improved to 108.4 males per 100 females by 2020. Urbanization is below the national average at 58%. Henan has the largest Christian population of any Chinese province by number and percentage (6.1% in 2012). People from Henan have historically faced discrimination within China.

### Economy and Industry
Henan has the 5th-largest provincial economy in China (CN¥5.88 trillion in 2021). It is a major agricultural producer, yielding over one-tenth of China's grain and one-quarter of its wheat. The province has a vast agricultural processing sector and is a national leader in livestock and edible fungus production. Industrially, it possesses all 41 of China's industrial categories, with strengths in electronic information, equipment manufacturing, and food processing. Henan is a global leader in superhard materials (synthetic diamonds) and wig manufacturing. New energy vehicle production has grown rapidly, from 66,000 units in 2021 to over 750,000 in 2025.

### Transportation and Geography
Henan's landscape features the North China Plain in the east and mountains (Taihang, Xionger, Funiu) in the west. The Yellow River flows through its center, historically causing floods but now controlled by dams and levees. The province is a central transportation hub, with a "米"-shaped high-speed rail network centered on Zhengzhou. By 2025, it had over 6,800 km of railways and nearly 9,000 km of expressways. Zhengzhou Airport is a major cargo hub with routes to over 30 countries.

### Culture and Education
Henan is the heartland of Chinese mythology, associated with figures like Nüwa, Fuxi, and the Yellow Emperor. It is the birthplace of Shaolin kung fu and Chen-style taijiquan. The province's literature has a long history, from the Classic of Poetry to modern writers like Liu Cixin. It hosts over 160 higher education institutions, with three cities ranking among the top 140 globally for scientific research output.

## Terms
- **Central Plains (Zhongyuan):** The historical name for the area around Henan, considered the core region of Chinese civilization.
- **Hukou:** China's household registration system, which ties social benefits to one's registered location.
- **Demographic transition model:** A model describing population change from high birth/death rates to low ones as a society develops.
- **People's commune:** A rural administrative and economic unit established during the Great Leap Forward.
- **Grand Canal:** A vast waterway system, extended to Luoyang during the Sui dynasty.
- **Zhongyuan Mandarin:** The dialect of Mandarin spoken across most of Henan province.
- **Nature Index:** A database tracking research output by city and institution.

## Debates and Open questions
- The identification of the Erlitou culture with the legendary Xia dynasty is controversial.
- The exact death toll of the 1887 Yellow River flood is debated, with estimates ranging from 930,000 to 7 million.
- The death toll from the 1975 Banqiao Dam collapse is uncertain, with unofficial estimates ranging from 26,000 to 230,000.
- Conflicting data exists on Henan's gender ratio, with some studies suggesting a much higher imbalance than official statistics report.
