# Omdurman

## Overview
Omdurman is a major city in Sudan, the country's second most populous, located on the west bank of the Nile opposite the capital, Khartoum. It is historically significant as the former capital of the Mahdist State and the site of a pivotal 1898 battle that established British control over Sudan.

## Timeline
- **c. 1885** — After the siege of Khartoum, the city grew rapidly as the Mahdist capital.
- **September 1898** — British forces under General Kitchener decisively defeated Mahdist forces in the Battle of Omdurman.
- **1899** — British forces defeated the Khalifa at the Battle of Umm Diwaykarat, securing control.
- **1899–1956** — Sudan was jointly governed by Great Britain and Egypt.
- **10 May 2008** — The Justice and Equality Movement engaged in heavy fighting with government forces in the city.
- **April 2023–May 2025** — The city was occupied by the Rapid Support Forces during the Sudanese civil war.

## Body

### History and Strategic Importance
Omdurman's rapid growth followed the 1885 siege of Khartoum and the death of the Mahdi. The city's strategic importance led to the 1898 Battle of Omdurman, where a British army of 20,000 equipped with modern weapons defeated over 52,000 Mahdist defenders. The battle resulted in at least 10,000 Sudanese casualties compared to fewer than 400 British. Following the victory, General Kitchener restored Khartoum as the capital. Omdurman remained a key urban center, connected to Khartoum and Khartoum Bahri by bridges over the Nile.

### Geography and Climate
The city features a hot arid climate with minimal annual precipitation, averaging just over 155 mm (6.1 in). It is one of the world's hottest major cities, with an average annual high temperature of 37.1 °C (99 °F). Temperatures routinely exceed 40 °C (104 °F) in summer, and no month has an average high below 30 °C (86 °F).

### Education and Infrastructure
Omdurman is a major educational hub with several public universities, including Karary University and Omdurman Islamic University, and private institutions like Ahfad University for Women. Transportation includes taxis, buses, tuk-tuks, trains, and Nile ferries. The city is served by Khartoum Airport, and a new international airport was completed south of the city in December 2025.

## Terms
- **Mahdist State**: A religious-political state established in Sudan in the late 19th century.
- **Khalifa**: The title for the successor to the Mahdi, held by Abdallahi ibn Muhammad.
- **Dervishes**: A term used in the source to describe the Mahdist fighters.
- **Maxim gun**: An early fully automatic machine gun used by the British.
- **Arid climate**: A climate characterized by very low precipitation.
- **Tuk-tuk**: A three-wheeled motorcycle used for local transport.
