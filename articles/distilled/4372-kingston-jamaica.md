# Kingston, Jamaica

## Overview
Kingston is the capital and largest city of Jamaica, located on the island's southeastern coast. It is the largest English-speaking city south of the United States in the Western Hemisphere and serves as the nation's primary economic, cultural, and political hub. The city's history is marked by its founding after a devastating earthquake, rapid commercial growth, and periods of social and political turbulence.

## Timeline
- **1692** — Kingston founded to house survivors of the earthquake that destroyed Port Royal.
- **1703** — Port Royal's destruction by fire accelerates Kingston's growth.
- **1716** — Kingston becomes Jamaica's largest town and trade center.
- **1872** — Government offices are officially transferred from Spanish Town to Kingston.
- **1907** — A major earthquake kills 800 people and destroys much of the historic city center.
- **1948** — The Mona campus of the University of the West Indies is founded in Kingston.
- **1966** — Kingston hosts the Commonwealth Games.
- **1980s** — Political shift towards market-oriented policies; major waterfront redevelopment.
- **2010** — Armed conflict between security forces and the Shower Posse drug cartel results in significant casualties.

## Body

### History and Founding
Kingston was founded on 22 July 1692 following the earthquake that devastated Port Royal. Survivors established a camp on the seafront, and the town began to grow significantly after Port Royal was further destroyed by fire in 1703. A grid plan was laid out to facilitate commerce, and by 1716, it was Jamaica's largest town. Although government offices were briefly considered for transfer in 1755, the official move from Spanish Town occurred in 1872. The city suffered another major earthquake in 1907, which led to a building height restriction. The 20th century saw the establishment of the University of the West Indies (1948), the hosting of the 1966 Commonwealth Games, and periods of political unrest and crime, including the 2010 Kingston unrest.

### Geography and Climate
Kingston is situated on the Liguanea plain, bordered by the Blue Mountains and the seventh-largest natural harbour in the world. The city experiences a tropical wet-and-dry climate, with a wet season from May to November. It lies in the rain shadow of the Blue Mountains, making it significantly drier than the island's windward side. Kingston is prone to earthquakes and hurricanes.

### Demographics and Society
The population is predominantly of African descent, with significant minority groups including East Indians, Chinese, Europeans, and Syrians/Lebanese. The city is religiously diverse, with various Christian denominations, the Rastafari movement, and communities of Hindus, Buddhists, Muslims, and Jews. Housing is marked by stark inequality, with affluent suburbs like Red Hills coexisting with squatter settlements that house 20% of the population.

### Economy and Infrastructure
Kingston is the core of Jamaica's economy, hosting most government ministries, financial institutions, and multinational headquarters. Key industries include tourism, apparel manufacturing, and shipping through its major seaport. The city is the island's main transportation hub, served by the Norman Manley International Airport and a network of roads, including Highway 2000. Public transport includes the Jamaica Urban Transit Company (JUTC) bus system.

## Terms
- **Corporate Area**: The combined metropolitan area of Kingston and St. Andrew parishes, administered by the Kingston and St. Andrew Corporation (KSAC).
- **Palisadoes**: A long sand spit that protects Kingston Harbour and connects Port Royal and the airport to the rest of the island.
- **Liguanea Plains**: The alluvial plain alongside the Hope River where Kingston is situated.
- **Kingston Harbour**: The seventh-largest natural harbour in the world, central to the city's trade.
- **Norman Manley International Airport**: Kingston's primary international airport.
- **JUTC**: The Jamaica Urban Transit Company, the public bus operator in Kingston.
- **Shower Posse**: A drug cartel involved in the 2010 armed conflict with Jamaican security forces.
- **KSAC**: The Kingston and St. Andrew Corporation, the local government body for the Corporate Area.
