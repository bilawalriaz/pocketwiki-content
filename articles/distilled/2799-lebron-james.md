# LeBron James

## Overview
LeBron James is an American professional basketball player widely considered one of the greatest athletes in modern history. His career, spanning over two decades, is defined by unparalleled longevity, statistical dominance, and a profound impact on the NBA's competitive and business landscape. He is the NBA's all-time leading scorer and a four-time champion, whose career choices have reshaped player empowerment and team-building strategies.

## Timeline
- **Dec 30, 1984** — Born in Akron, Ohio.
- **2003** — Selected 1st overall by the Cleveland Cavaliers; wins Rookie of the Year.
- **2010** — Announces move to the Miami Heat via "The Decision."
- **2012** — Wins first NBA championship and Finals MVP with the Heat.
- **2014** — Returns to the Cleveland Cavaliers.
- **2016** — Leads Cleveland to its first major sports title in 52 years, overcoming a 3-1 Finals deficit.
- **2018** — Signs with the Los Angeles Lakers.
- **2020** — Wins fourth championship and Finals MVP with the Lakers, becoming the first to do so with three different teams.
- **Feb 7, 2023** — Passes Kareem Abdul-Jabbar as the NBA's all-time leading scorer.
- **Oct 22, 2024** — Plays in his first NBA game alongside his son Bronny, the first father-son duo to do so.
- **Jul 24, 2026** — Signs with the Philadelphia 76ers, calling it his "last decision."

## Body

### Early Life and High School Stardom
Born to a teenage mother in Akron, James gained stability by living with a youth football coach's family. He became a national basketball phenomenon at St. Vincent–St. Mary High School, where his teams won three state titles in four years. He was the first junior to be named Gatorade National Player of the Year and appeared on the cover of *Sports Illustrated* as an underclassman. His senior year was marked by controversies over gifts (a Hummer H2 and throwback jerseys) that temporarily jeopardized his eligibility.

### Cleveland Cavaliers (First Stint: 2003–2010)
Drafted first overall, James won Rookie of the Year and quickly became a superstar. He led Cleveland to its first NBA Finals in 2007, where they were swept by the Spurs. After winning back-to-back MVP awards (2009, 2010), he departed for Miami as a free agent in 2010, a move announced in the controversial television special *The Decision*.

### Miami Heat (2010–2014)
Joining Dwyane Wade and Chris Bosh to form a "superteam," James faced intense media scrutiny. After a Finals loss in 2011, he won consecutive championships in 2012 and 2013, earning Finals MVP both years. His game evolved significantly under coach Erik Spoelstra, improving his post play and shot selection. The Heat lost in the 2014 Finals to the Spurs.

### Cleveland Cavaliers (Second Stint: 2014–2018)
James returned to Cleveland and delivered the city's first major sports championship in 52 years by leading a historic comeback from a 3–1 deficit against the 73-win Golden State Warriors in the 2016 Finals. He reached three more consecutive Finals with Cleveland, losing to the Warriors in 2017 and 2018.

### Los Angeles Lakers (2018–2026)
After a first-year injury, James led the Lakers to the 2020 championship, becoming the first Finals MVP with three different franchises. In 2023, he became the NBA's all-time leading scorer. His longevity produced numerous records, including becoming the first player to score 40,000 career points and the all-time leader in games played. He played alongside his son Bronny in the 2024-25 season.

### Philadelphia 76ers (2026–Present)
In July 2026, James signed with the 76ers, marking the final chapter of his career.

### Player Profile and Legacy
James is a 6'9", 250-pound forward known for his athleticism, versatility, and elite playmaking. He ranks fourth in NBA career assists and is considered one of the greatest passers in history. His career has been defined by a debate over his status as the greatest player of all time, often compared to Michael Jordan. His free agency decisions, starting with *The Decision*, are credited with catalyzing the era of player empowerment and "superteams."

### Off-Court Impact
James is a global icon and businessman, becoming the first active NBA player to reach billionaire status. His production company, SpringHill, and media platform, Uninterrupted, extend his influence. He is a noted philanthropist, founding the I Promise School in Akron, and has been vocal on social and political issues, including racial justice and voting rights.

## Terms
- **Prep-to-pro**: A player who enters the NBA directly from high school, bypassing college.
- **Superteam**: A team with multiple established All-Star players, often formed through free agency.
- **Triple-double**: A single-game performance in which a player records double-digit totals in three of five statistical categories (points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks).
- **Finals MVP**: The award given to the most valuable player of the NBA Finals series.
- **Player empowerment**: A modern NBA trend where star players exert significant influence over team decisions, including trades and free agency destinations.
- **All-NBA Team**: An annual honor given to the league's best players at each position, with First, Second, and Third Team designations.
- **Chase-down block**: A defensive play where a trailing player blocks a shot from behind, often in transition.

## Debates and Open Questions
- **Greatest of All Time (GOAT)**: The central debate of James's legacy is whether he surpasses Michael Jordan as the greatest basketball player. Polls often show a generational divide, with older fans favoring Jordan and younger fans favoring James.
- **Finals Record**: Critics point to James's 4-6 record in the NBA Finals as a mark against his legacy, while supporters argue his teams were often undermatched and that his individual performances were exceptional.
- **Impact of "The Decision"**: The manner of his 2010 departure from Cleveland remains a point of criticism, though James later expressed regret. It fundamentally changed how star players approach free agency.
