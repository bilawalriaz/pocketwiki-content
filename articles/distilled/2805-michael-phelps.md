# Michael Phelps

## Overview
Michael Phelps is an American former competitive swimmer, widely regarded as the greatest swimmer of all time. He is the most decorated Olympian in history, holding the records for most Olympic gold medals (23) and most Olympic medals overall (28). His career spanned five Olympic Games from 2000 to 2016, during which he dominated multiple swimming disciplines and set numerous world records.

## Timeline
- **June 30, 1985** — Born in Baltimore, Maryland.
- **2000** — Competes at the Sydney Olympics at age 15, finishing 5th in the 200m butterfly.
- **March 30, 2001** — Becomes the youngest male to set a world record in swimming (200m butterfly).
- **2004** — Wins 6 gold and 2 bronze medals at the Athens Olympics, tying the record for most medals at a single Games.
- **2007** — Wins 7 gold medals at the World Championships, tying Mark Spitz's long-standing record.
- **August 17, 2008** — Wins his 8th gold medal at the Beijing Olympics, breaking Spitz's record for most golds at a single Games.
- **2009** — Sets a world record in the 100m butterfly (49.82), becoming the first man under 50 seconds.
- **August 4, 2012** — Surpasses Larisa Latynina to become the all-time record holder for most Olympic medals.
- **August 12, 2016** — Announces his second retirement after winning 5 gold and 1 silver medal at the Rio Olympics.

## Body

### Early Life and Training
Phelps began swimming at age seven, partly as an outlet for his energy after being diagnosed with ADHD. He trained under coach Bob Bowman from age 11, a partnership that lasted throughout his career. Phelps qualified for his first Olympics in 2000 at age 15, becoming the youngest male swimmer on a U.S. Olympic team in 68 years.

### Rise to Dominance (2001-2004)
Phelps became the youngest male to set a world record in 2001. He broke multiple world records leading up to the 2004 Athens Olympics, where he won six gold and two bronze medals. This performance tied the record for most medals at a single Olympics and established him as the sport's new superstar.

### The Beijing Peak (2008)
At the 2008 Beijing Olympics, Phelps achieved his career pinnacle by winning eight gold medals, breaking Mark Spitz's 1972 record. He set world records in all eight events. His victory in the 100m butterfly was by just 0.01 seconds, and his final gold in the 4x100m medley relay secured the historic eighth title.

### Later Career and Comeback (2009-2016)
After a brief retirement following the 2012 London Olympics, where he won four gold and two silver medals, Phelps returned in 2014. At the 2016 Rio Olympics, he won five gold and one silver medals, becoming the oldest male champion in Olympic swimming history at age 31. He retired as the most decorated Olympian ever.

### Personal Life and Philanthropy
Phelps has spoken about his struggles with ADHD and depression. He founded the Michael Phelps Foundation after the 2008 Olympics to promote swimming and healthy lifestyles. He has had two DUI arrests, in 2004 and 2014, the latter resulting in a six-month suspension from competition.

## Terms
*   **Long course:** Competition in a 50-meter pool, the standard for Olympic and World Championship events.
*   **Short course:** Competition in a 25-meter pool.
*   **Individual medley (IM):** A race combining all four competitive strokes (butterfly, backstroke, breaststroke, freestyle) in a set order.
*   **FINA:** Fédération Internationale de Natation, the international federation recognized by the International Olympic Committee for administering international aquatic competitions.
*   **ADHD:** Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder, a neurodevelopmental condition Phelps has publicly discussed having.
*   **World record:** The fastest time ever officially recorded in a specific swimming event.
*   **Olympic record:** The fastest time ever recorded at an Olympic Games in a specific event.

## Debates and Open Questions
*   **The "super suit" era:** Phelps's coach Bob Bowman questioned the fairness of polyurethane swimsuits after Phelps lost the 200m freestyle at the 2009 World Championships to a swimmer wearing one. The controversy led to suit regulations being changed.
*   **Relay selection controversy:** At the 2004 Olympic Trials, Phelps was selected for the 4x100m freestyle relay despite not competing in the 100m freestyle event at the trials, drawing criticism from teammate Gary Hall Jr.
*   **The 100m butterfly finish in 2008:** Phelps's victory by 0.01 seconds over Milorad Čavić in Beijing prompted a protest from the Serbian delegation. The result was confirmed by video analysis, but the initial refusal to release underwater photos raised questions.
