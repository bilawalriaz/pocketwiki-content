# Songhua River

## Overview
The Songhua River is one of China's primary rivers and the longest tributary of the Amur River. It is a vital waterway for the northeastern provinces of Jilin and Heilongjiang, supporting major cities like Harbin and Jilin, but its history includes significant pollution events that have caused regional crises.

## Body
### Geography and Course
The Songhua River rises south of Heaven Lake near the China–North Korea border. It flows north through Jilin province, interrupted by the Baishan, Hongshi, and Fengman hydroelectric dams. The Fengman Dam forms a lake stretching 62 kilometers. Below this dam, the river is called the Second Songhua. It flows north and then northwest until it is joined by its largest tributary, the Nen River, near Da'an. This confluence creates the main Songhua River, which then turns east through Harbin. Further downstream, it is joined by the Ashi and Hulan rivers before flowing past Jiamusi and south of the Lesser Xing'an Range. It finally joins the Amur River at Tongjiang, Heilongjiang.

The river's course is characterized by extreme flatness in the Northeast China Plain, causing it to meander and form oxbow lakes. It freezes from late November until March, with highest flows during the spring thaw from mountain snowmelt. The river is navigable by medium-sized ships up to Harbin and by smaller craft to Jilin and Qiqihar on the Nen River.

### History and Human Impact
The Songhua River has been subject to major pollution incidents. In November 2005, a benzene spill contaminated the river, forcing the shutdown of Harbin's water supply. The pollution slick traveled 80 kilometers and eventually reached the Amur River on the China–Russia border. In July 2010, severe floods washed away thousands of barrels from chemical plants in Jilin City. Some barrels contained explosive materials like trimethylsilyl chloride and hexamethyldisiloxane. A minor flood also affected the river near Jilin in 2016.

## Terms
- **Tributary**: A river or stream that flows into a larger river or lake.
- **Oxbow lake**: A U-shaped lake that forms when a wide meander of a river is cut off, creating a free-standing body of water.
- **Hydroelectric dam**: A structure built across a river to generate electricity from the flow of water.
- **Meander**: A winding curve or bend in a river.
- **Discharge**: The volume of water flowing through a river channel per unit of time.
- **Reservoir**: An artificial lake created by a dam for storing water.
