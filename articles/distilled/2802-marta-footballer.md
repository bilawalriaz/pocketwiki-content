# Marta (footballer)

## Overview
Marta Vieira da Silva, known mononymously as Marta, is a Brazilian professional footballer widely regarded as the greatest female footballer of all time. A prolific forward, she is Brazil's all-time top scorer and holds the record for most career goals in the Women's World Cup. Her career, spanning over two decades, has been marked by individual brilliance, numerous titles, and a significant impact on the global profile of women's football.

## Timeline
- **19 Feb 1986** — Born in Dois Riachos, Alagoas, Brazil.
- **2000** — Starts professional career with Vasco da Gama.
- **2004** — Wins UEFA Women's Cup with Umeå IK; wins Olympic silver medal with Brazil.
- **2007** — Wins Golden Ball and Golden Boot at FIFA Women's World Cup; Brazil finishes runner-up.
- **2009** — Wins WPS MVP with Los Angeles Sol; wins Copa Libertadores with Santos.
- **2010** — Wins WPS Championship and MVP with FC Gold Pride.
- **2012** — Returns to Sweden, helps Tyresö FF win Damallsvenskan.
- **2017** — Joins Orlando Pride in the NWSL.
- **2019** — Becomes first player to score at five FIFA World Cups.
- **2024** — Wins NWSL Shield and Championship with Orlando Pride; wins third Olympic silver medal.
- **2025** — Comes out of international retirement to help Brazil win Copa América Femenina.

## Body

### Club Career
Marta began her career in Brazil before moving to Sweden's Umeå IK in 2004, where she won the UEFA Women's Cup and multiple league titles. She later starred in the U.S. Women's Professional Soccer (WPS) league, winning back-to-back MVP awards with the Los Angeles Sol and FC Gold Pride. After WPS folded, she returned to Sweden with Tyresö FF and FC Rosengård, winning more league championships. In 2017, she joined Orlando Pride in the National Women's Soccer League (NWSL), where she became captain and, in 2024, won both the NWSL Shield and Championship.

### International Career
Marta debuted for Brazil in 2002 and quickly became their talisman. She led Brazil to the 2007 Women's World Cup final, winning both the Golden Ball and Golden Boot. She has won three Olympic silver medals (2004, 2008, 2024) and four Copa América Femenina titles. She holds the record for most Women's World Cup goals (17) and is the first player, male or female, to score at five World Cups and five consecutive Olympic Games. After initially retiring post-2024 Olympics, she returned to help Brazil win the 2025 Copa América.

### Style of Play and Legacy
A short yet sturdy player, Marta is renowned for her quick feet, technical skill, and pace. Often compared to male legends like Pelé, she has played as an offensive midfielder, winger, and striker. Her leadership and advocacy have elevated women's football globally. In 2024, FIFA established the "Marta Award" for the most beautiful goal of the year, which she won.

## Terms
- **Damallsvenskan**: The top division of women's football in Sweden.
- **WPS (Women's Professional Soccer)**: A top-tier U.S. women's league active from 2009 to 2012.
- **NWSL (National Women's Soccer League)**: The current top-tier professional women's soccer league in the United States.
- **Golden Ball**: Award given to the best player at a FIFA tournament.
- **Golden Boot**: Award given to the top goalscorer at a FIFA tournament.
- **Copa Libertadores de Fútbol Femenino**: The premier women's club football tournament in South America.
- **NWSL Shield**: Awarded to the team with the best regular-season record in the NWSL.
- **ACL tear**: A serious knee injury involving the anterior cruciate ligament.

## Debates and Open Questions
The source material presents Marta's status as the greatest female footballer of all time as a widely held view ("regarded by many") rather than a settled, objective fact, implying an ongoing debate. Her career is also defined by a series of major tournament final losses (2007 World Cup, 2004, 2008, 2024 Olympics), which contrasts with her immense individual success and club achievements.
