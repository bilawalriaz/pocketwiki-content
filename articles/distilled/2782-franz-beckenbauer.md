# Franz Beckenbauer

## Overview
Franz Beckenbauer (1945–2024) was a German football legend, widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time. He revolutionized the sport by inventing the modern attacking sweeper (libero) role and achieved unparalleled success as both a player and manager, winning the FIFA World Cup in both capacities.

- **1994** — Became president of Bayern Munich.
- **2006** — Chaired the organizing committee for the FIFA World Cup in Germany.
- **7 Jan 2024** — Died in Salzburg, Austria.

## Body

### Playing Career and Innovation
Beckenbauer began as a midfielder but became famous as a centre-back for Bayern Munich and West Germany. He is credited with inventing the modern sweeper (libero) role, a defender who proactively joins the attack. He won three consecutive European Cups (1974–76) with Bayern and three Bundesliga titles in a row (1972–74). Internationally, he earned 103 caps, played in three World Cups, and won the 1972 European Championship and 1974 World Cup as captain.

### Managerial and Administrative Career
After retiring, Beckenbauer managed West Germany to the 1986 World Cup final and victory in 1990, becoming one of only three men to win the World Cup as both player and manager. He later managed Bayern Munich, winning the Bundesliga (1994) and UEFA Cup (1996). As president of Bayern Munich (1994–2009), he oversaw significant success. He also chaired the organizing committee for Germany's successful 2006 World Cup bid.

### Financial Controversies
Beckenbauer's post-playing career was marred by financial investigations. He was banned and fined by FIFA for not cooperating with inquiries into the 2018/2022 World Cup bids. Separate investigations concerned a payment related to South Africa's 2010 World Cup bid and undisclosed income from a German Football Association sponsorship deal. A criminal investigation into fraud and money laundering related to the 2006 World Cup was closed in 2020 due to the statute of limitations expiring.

## Terms
- **Libero (Sweeper):** A defensive player positioned behind the main defensive line, tasked with intercepting attacks and, in Beckenbauer's innovative style, initiating offensive plays.
- **Ballon d'Or:** An annual award given to the best football player in Europe; Beckenbauer won it twice (1972, 1976).
- **FIFA World Cup:** The premier international football tournament contested by men's national teams.
- **European Cup / UEFA Champions League:** The top-tier club football competition in Europe.
- **Bundesliga:** The top professional football league in Germany.
- **NASL (North American Soccer League):** The top professional soccer league in the United States and Canada during the 1970s and early 1980s.
- **Statute of Limitations:** A legal principle that sets the maximum time after an event within which legal proceedings may be initiated.

## Debates and Open Questions
- **Origin of Nickname:** The popular story that his nickname "der Kaiser" (the Emperor) originated from a photo with a bust of Emperor Franz Joseph I is disputed. An alternative account links it to a defiant act in a 1969 cup final.
- **2006 World Cup Allegations:** The full circumstances of payments and potential bribery related to Germany's 2006 World Cup bid remain unclear, as the official investigation was closed without a verdict.
