# Special Interest Group on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining

ACM SIGKDD is the Association for Computing Machinery's Special Interest Group devoted to knowledge discovery and data mining (KDD), the practice of extracting patterns and useful knowledge from large datasets. Its flagship event is the annual SIGKDD International Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining, which publishes proceedings through ACM and is widely regarded as the most influential forum for data mining research.

The conference traces back to KDD workshops attached to AAAI conferences: Gregory I. Piatetsky-Shapiro organized them in 1989, 1991, and 1993, and Usama Fayyad in 1994. Demand grew quickly. The KDD conference has been held every year since 1995, and SIGKDD was formally chartered as an official ACM Special Interest Group in 1998.

The conference rotates across continents. Recent host cities include San Diego (2011, 2020), Beijing (2012), Chicago (2013), New York City (2014), Sydney (2015), San Francisco (2016), Halifax (2017), London (2018), Anchorage (2019), virtual (2021), Washington D.C. (2022), Long Beach (2023), Barcelona (2024), and Toronto (2025).

SIGKDD sets a high bar for publication. Papers must emphasize innovative research in data mining, knowledge discovery, and large-scale analytics, with theoretical foundations and novel modeling or algorithmic approaches especially welcome. Incremental work without significant advances is explicitly discouraged. Reviewers, drawn from leading experts unaffiliated with the authors, judge each submission on novelty, technical quality, potential impact, clarity, and rigor of experimental methods. In 2014, more than 2,600 authors from at least fourteen countries submitted over 1,000 papers; 151 were accepted, a 14.6% rate, slightly tighter than the 15–25% typical of other top computer science venues. Between 1994 and 2015, 4,489 SIGKDD papers accumulated 112,570 citations across 3,303 venues, 56% of which were themselves top-25 venues. The conference also holds the highest A* rating from the Computing Research and Education (CORE) organization.

SIGKDD recognizes excellence through the annual Innovation Award and Service Award. The conference itself grants two research prizes: the Best Research Track Paper Award for the strongest scholarly contribution, judged by conference chairs and an awards panel, and the Best Student Paper Award, restricted to papers whose first author is a student. Past recipients include Foster Provost, Pedro Domingos, Padhraic Smyth, Jon Kleinberg, Christos Faloutsos, Jure Leskovec, and Yehuda Koren, reflecting the field's strong presence in both academia and large technology firms.

Beyond the conference, SIGKDD runs the annual KDD Cup data mining competition for students and practitioners, and publishes SIGKDD Explorations, a biannual journal founded by Fayyad in June 1999. Successive Editors-in-Chief have been Fayyad (1999–2002), Sunita Sarawagi (2003–2006), Ramakrishnan Srikant (2006–2007), Osmar R. Zaiane (2008–2010), Bart Goethals (2010–2013), and Charu Aggarwal (2014–).

Governance has been shaped by recurring figures. The 1998 founding board included Won Kim as Chair, Rakesh Agrawal as Secretary/Treasurer, and directors Fayyad, Piatetsky-Shapiro, Daryl Pregibon, and Padhraic Smyth. Kim led from 1998 to 2005, followed by Piatetsky-Shapiro (2005–2009) and Fayyad (2009–2013), with Bing Liu serving as Chair from 2013 onward. The WSDM conference accepts fewer papers than the higher-ranked SIGIR in information retrieval, illustrating that a lower acceptance rate does not always signal a stronger venue.
